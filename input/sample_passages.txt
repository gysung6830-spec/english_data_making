Many people believe that talent is fixed, but research suggests otherwise. When students are praised for effort rather than ability, they tend to take on harder challenges. However, when they are told they are simply smart, they often avoid tasks that might reveal weakness. For instance, a study at a large school found that a growth mindset improved grades over time. Therefore, the way we give feedback matters. Teachers who emphasize progress help students persist. In conclusion, effort-based praise builds resilience that ability-based praise cannot.
