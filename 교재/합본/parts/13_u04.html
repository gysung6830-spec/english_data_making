수일치

수식어구 · 도치 구문 실전 적용

1. 대원칙 — 주어의 '핵'에 맞춘다

주어–동사 수일치
동사의 단수/복수는 주어의 핵(head noun)에 맞춘다. 주어와 동사 사이의 수식어구(거품)는 무시.
The box of old letters is on the table. (핵 box)
The students who study hard pass the exam. (관계절 무시)

2. 조심할 주어들

주어수예
동명사/부정사/명사절단수Reading books is fun.
each/every, either/neither of단수Each of them has …
most/some/half of + Nof 뒤 명사Most of the water is / Most of them are
a number of + 복수N복수(많은)A number of cars were …
the number of + 복수N단수(~의 수)The number of cars was …
the + 형용사복수The rich are …
상관접속사 주어 — "가까운 쪽" 규칙
both A and B → 복수 / either A or B · neither A nor B · not only A but also B · A as well as B → B(뒤)에 일치.
Not only you but also he is wrong. / Either you or I am right.

3. 도치 구문 — 진짜 주어는 동사 뒤

유형예
There + be + 주어There are many reasons.
장소·방향 부사구 도치On the hill stands an old castle.
부정어 도치Never have I seen such a thing.
Only + 부사(구) 도치Only then did he realize the truth.
▶ 연습문제 (16문항)
유형 A. 어법 선택 [1~7]
  1. The list of the winners ( was / were ) posted online.
  2. Each of the rooms ( has / have ) its own bathroom.
  3. Not only the players but also the coach ( was / were ) upset.
  4. The number of tourists ( has / have ) increased sharply.
  5. Reading books before bed ( help / helps ) you sleep.
  6. Most of the money ( was / were ) spent on rent.
  7. A number of students ( is / are ) waiting outside.
유형 B. 도치 구문 수일치 [8~11]
  1. There ( is / are ) a pen and two books on the desk.
  2. Under the tree ( was / were ) two sleeping cats.
  3. Never ( I have / have I ) seen such beauty.
  4. On the wall ( hang / hangs ) a large painting.
유형 C. 밑줄 O/X — 틀리면 수정 [12~14]
  1. Neither of the answers are correct.
  2. The rich is not always happy.
  3. Ten years is a long time to wait.
유형 D. 괄호 동사를 알맞은 형태로 [15~16]
  1. Either the teacher or the students (be) ______ mistaken.
  2. The quality of the products (determine) ______ our reputation.
