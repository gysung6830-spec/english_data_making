UNIT 3

분사 및 태

능동 vs 수동 · 분사구문 패턴 체화

1. 태의 원리 — 주어가 '하는가' vs '당하는가'

능동 vs 수동
주어가 동작을 직접 하면 능동, 당하면 수동. 수동은 be + p.p. (+ by).
The chef cooked the meal. → The meal was cooked by the chef.
태 판별 3원칙
① 주어가 행위자면 능동, 대상이면 수동. ② 목적어가 있으면 능동. ③ 자동사는 수동 불가(happen, occur, appear, arrive, exist, seem…).
✕ The accident was happened. → ○ The accident happened.

2. 분사 — 현재분사(능동) vs 과거분사(수동)

분사의미예
현재분사 -ing능동·진행 "~하는"a running dog / the exciting game
과거분사 p.p.수동·완료 "~된/당한"a stolen bike / the excited fans
감정분사 — 유발(-ing) vs 느낌(-ed)
감정을 주면 -ing(boring, surprising), 감정을 느끼면 -ed(bored, surprised). 사람 주어는 대개 -ed.
The movie was boring, so the kids were bored.

3. 분사구문 만드는 3단계

①
접속사를 지운다(의미 강조 시 남김).
②
주절과 주어가 같으면 부사절 주어를 지운다.
③
동사를 -ing(능동) 또는 (being) p.p.(수동)로 바꾼다.
When she saw the police, she ran. → Seeing the police, she ran.
Because it was written in haste, … → (Being) Written in haste, …
현수분사(dangling) 주의
분사의 의미상 주어 = 주절 주어. ✕ Walking home, the rain started. (걷는 주체가 rain이 아님)
with + 명사 + 분사(부대상황): with her eyes closed(수동) / with his dog following(능동).
▶ 연습문제 (16문항)
유형 A. 어법 선택(분사) [1~6]
  1. The results were quite ( surprising / surprised ).
  2. The building ( damaging / damaged ) by fire was demolished.
  3. The audience looked ( boring / bored ) during the speech.
  4. Look at the ( breaking / broken ) glass on the floor.
  5. ( Finishing / Finished ) his homework, he went out.
  6. She sat there with her arms ( folding / folded ).
유형 B. 능동/수동 태 선택 [7~10]
  1. The bridge ( built / was built ) in 1920 is still used.
  2. A strange thing ( happened / was happened ) to me.
  3. This song ( writes / was written ) by a famous composer.
  4. The problem ( discussing / being discussed ) now is serious.
유형 C. 밑줄 O/X — 틀리면 수정 [11~13]
  1. Written in easy English, I could read the book.
  2. The news was very shocking to everyone.
  3. The letter wrote in French was hard to read.
유형 D. 분사구문으로 전환 [14~16]
  1. As I didn't know what to say, I remained silent.
  2. Because he was tired, he went to bed early.
  3. After she had finished dinner, she washed the dishes.
