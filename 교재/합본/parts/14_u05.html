UNIT 5

병렬구조와 비교구문

등위·상관접속사 병렬 · 비교 기출공식

1. 병렬 — 연결된 것은 '같은 급'

병렬(Parallelism)
and·but·or로 연결되는 요소는 문법적으로 같은 형태(명사↔명사, to부정사↔to부정사, 절↔절).
She likes swimming, hiking, and to run.
→ swimming, hiking, and running. (동명사로 통일)
상관접속사 병렬 — A·B 형태 일치
both A and B / not only A but also B / either A or B / neither A nor B / not A but B / A as well as B.
not only smart but also kind (형용사↔형용사)

2. 비교의 세 형태 & 대상 일치

형태공식예
원급as + 원급 + asas tall as his father
비교급-er / more + thantaller than me
최상급the + -est / mostthe tallest
비교 대상도 병렬 — that/those
비교하는 두 대상은 대등해야 한다. 반복 회피: 단수 that, 복수 those.
✕ milder than Canada → ○ milder than that of Canada.

3. 자주 나오는 비교 공식

공식의미
much/far/even/still + 비교급"훨씬" 강조 (very 비교급 ✕)
the 비교급 ~, the 비교급 ~"~할수록 더 …"
배수 + as ~ as / 비교급 thantwice as large as
비교급 and 비교급"점점 더"
superior/inferior/senior/prior to라틴 비교(than ✕)
▶ 연습문제 (16문항)
유형 A. 병렬 — 어법 선택 [1~5]
  1. He is good at singing, dancing, and ( act / acting ).
  2. She is not only a teacher but also ( a novelist / writes novels ).
  3. To save money is wiser than ( to waste / wasting ) it.
  4. I want either to stay home or ( go / to go ) out.
  5. The job requires patience, skill, and ( creative / creativity ).
유형 B. 비교 — 어법 선택 [6~11]
  1. The population of China is larger than ( India / that of India ).
  2. This problem is ( very / much ) more difficult than the last.
  3. Your product is superior ( than / to ) theirs.
  4. He runs twice as ( fast / faster ) as I do.
  5. The ears of a rabbit are longer than ( that / those ) of a cat.
  6. The ( harder / hardest ) you try, the better you get.
유형 C. 밑줄 O/X — 틀리면 수정 [12~14]
  1. This car is very faster than that one.
  2. He is senior than me by three years.
  3. Nothing is so precious as time.
유형 D. 지시대로 문장 완성 [15~16]
  1. "점점 더워지고 있다"를 비교급 반복으로: It is getting ______.
  2. 최상급 대용(비교급): No other mountain is ______ than Everest. (high)
