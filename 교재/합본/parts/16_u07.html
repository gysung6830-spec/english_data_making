UNIT 7

형용사 vs 부사

품사 혼동을 끊어내는 위치 분석법

1. 역할로 구분한다

품사자리 / 역할예
형용사명사 수식 / 보어(2·5형식)a happy boy / She is happy / made me happy
부사동사·형용사·부사·문장 수식(보어 ✕)run fast / very tall / Sadly, he left
위치 분석법
명사 앞 · be/감각동사 뒤 보어 → 형용사. 동사·형용사·부사를 꾸미기만 → 부사.
✕ She sings beautiful → sings beautifully.

2. 감각·상태동사 + 형용사(2형식)

look/feel/smell/taste/sound/seem/become + 형용사
보어로 형용사를 취한다. This soup tastes good(✕ well). / You look tired(✕ tiredly).

3. 헷갈리는 부사 & enough

-ly 없는 부사-ly 붙은 다른 뜻
hard 열심히hardly 거의 ~않다
late 늦게lately 최근에
near 가까이nearly 거의
high 높이highly 매우
enough 위치
형용사·부사는 뒤(old enough), 명사는 앞(enough money).
▶ 연습문제 (16문항)
유형 A. 형용사/부사 선택 [1~7]
  1. The children were playing ( quiet / quietly ) in the yard.
  2. These flowers smell ( sweet / sweetly ).
  3. He speaks English fluently and ( accurate / accurately ).
  4. She looked ( happy / happily ) at the good news.
  5. The team performed ( good / well ) in the finals.
  6. Drive ( careful / carefully ) on icy roads.
  7. The cake tastes ( wonderful / wonderfully ).
유형 B. hard/hardly 류 [8~11]
  1. I could ( hard / hardly ) believe my eyes.
  2. He has been very busy ( late / lately ).
  3. She works ( hard / hardly ) every day.
  4. It is a ( high / highly ) recommended book.
유형 C. enough 위치·어순 [12~13]
  1. She is ( enough old / old enough ) to drive.
  2. We don't have ( money enough / enough money ).
유형 D. 밑줄 O/X — 틀리면 수정 [14~16]
  1. He drove very careful and slow on the road.
  2. The soup smells deliciously.
  3. She sings beautiful and dances gracefully.
