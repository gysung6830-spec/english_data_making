UNIT 6

대명사와 대동사

지칭 오류를 잡아내는 결정적 트레이닝

1. 대명사 — 선행사와 수·성 일치

지칭 일치 원칙
대명사는 가리키는 선행 명사와 수·성이 일치. 시험은 단수 명사를 they/their로 받는 오류를 즐겨 낸다.
A company should protect their employees.
A company(단수) → its employees.

2. 재귀·지시·부정대명사

종류쓰임 / 예
재귀대명사 -self주어=목적어(She hurt herself) / 강조(I did it myself)
that / those반복 명사 회피: longer than those of a cat
it vs oneit=바로 그것 / one=같은 종류의 하나
one / another / other / the other(s)
· 둘: one ~ the other · 셋 이상: one ~ another ~ the others
· 막연한 일부: others · 나머지 전부(특정): the others

3. 대동사 — 동사(구) 반복 회피

대동사(pro-verb)
일반동사는 do/does/did, be·조동사·완료 have는 그 동사 반복.
He runs faster than I do. / She is happier than she was.
So / Neither 도치
긍정 동의 So + (대)동사 + 주어("So do I"), 부정 동의 Neither + (대)동사 + 주어("Neither can I").
▶ 연습문제 (16문항)
유형 A. 어법 선택 [1~6]
  1. Every passenger must fasten ( their / his or her ) seatbelt.
  2. The weather here is milder than ( that / it ) of London.
  3. I lost my umbrella, so I bought ( it / one ).
  4. She cut ( her / herself ) while cooking.
  5. He speaks English better than his brother ( does / speaks ).
  6. The government raised ( its / their ) taxes.
유형 B. one/another/other 등 [7~10]
  1. I have two dogs: one is white and ( another / the other ) is black.
  2. Some like coffee; ( other / others ) prefer tea.
  3. There are three colors: one red, ( another / the other ) blue, and the last green.
  4. These shoes are too small; show me ( another / other ) pair.
유형 C. So/Neither 응답 [11~13]
  1. "I don't like horror films." — "( So / Neither ) do I."
  2. "I can swim well." — "( So can I / So do I )."
  3. "I have finished." — "( So have I / So did I )."
유형 D. 밑줄 O/X — 틀리면 수정 [14~16]
  1. Everyone raised their hand. (규범 문법)
  2. The team celebrated their victory. (단일 집합)
  3. The climate of Seoul is colder than Tokyo.
