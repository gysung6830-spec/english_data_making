UNIT 10

전범위 누적 최종모의고사

1강~9강 전 범위 통합 25문항 · 권장 30분
Part A. 어법상 알맞은 것을 고르시오 [1~12]
  1. The scientist studying rare birds ( has / having ) published many papers. (1강)
  2. This is the reason ( why / which ) she resigned so suddenly. (2강)
  3. The bridge ( building / built ) in 1920 is still in use. (3강)
  4. Neither the manager nor the workers ( was / were ) satisfied. (4강)
  5. The weather in Seoul is colder than ( London / that of London ). (5강)
  6. Each student must submit ( their / his or her ) own report. (6강)
  7. The soup smells ( wonderful / wonderfully ) today. (7강)
  8. ( Despite / Although ) the heavy traffic, we arrived on time. (8강)
  9. My coach made me ( run / to run ) ten laps. (9강)
  10. Only after the exam ( I realized / did I realize ) my mistake. (4·9강)
  11. The book ( which / what ) I borrowed was fascinating. (2강)
  12. She is not only talented but also ( works hard / hardworking ). (5강)
Part B. 밑줄 친 부분이 틀린 곳을 찾아 고치시오 [13~19]
  1. Walking along the beach, the sunset was breathtaking. (3강)
  2. The number of applicants have doubled this year. (4강)
  3. She enjoys reading, painting, and to travel. (5강)
  4. I have two dogs; one is white and another is black. (6강)
  5. He drove very careful and slow on the icy road. (7강)
  6. In spite of it was raining, the match went on. (8강)
  7. My parents let me to stay up late on weekends. (9강)
Part C. 지시에 따라 문장을 완성하시오 [20~25]
  1. 관계사로 연결: I met a woman. + Her son teaches at my school. (2강)
  2. 분사구문으로: Because he was tired, he went to bed early. (3강)
  3. 가주어 구문 완성: ______ is difficult ______ (master) a foreign language. (9강)
  4. 강조구문으로(in 2010): We first met in 2010. (9강)
  5. 어법 판단·수정: The accident was happened near my house. (3강)
  6. 두 문장 결합(정동사 유지·접속사): The train arrived. + Everyone cheered. (1강)
정답 & 해설 — Part A
#정답해설
1has주절 정동사 필요(studying은 분사).
2why완전한 절 + reason → 관계부사.
3built다리가 '지어진'(수동) 분사.
4wereneither A nor B → B(workers) 일치.
5that of London날씨끼리 비교.
6his or hereach student(단수).
7wonderfulsmell + 형용사 보어.
8Despite뒤 명사구 → 전치사.
9run사역 make + 원형.
10did I realizeOnly + 부사구 문두 → 도치.
11which선행사 the book 있음(what 불가).
12hardworkingtalented(형용사)와 병렬.
Part B
#수정해설
13주어를 사람으로: …, I found the sunset breathtaking현수분사 오류.
14have → hasthe number of ~ 단수.
15to travel → traveling동명사 병렬.
16another → the other둘 중 나머지.
17and slow → and slowly부사 병렬(careful→carefully).
18it was raining → the rain전치사 뒤 절 불가.
19to stay → stay사역 let + 원형.
Part C
#정답 예시해설
20I met a woman whose son teaches at my school.소유격 whose.
21(Being) Tired, he went to bed early.접속사·주어 제거 후 분사.
22It is difficult to master a foreign language.가주어–진주어.
23It was in 2010 that we first met.It ~ that 강조.
24was happened → happenedhappen은 자동사(수동 불가).
25The train arrived, and everyone cheered.정동사 2개 → 접속사 연결.
