고난도 킬러 패턴 집중 공략

1. 5형식 — 목적격 보어 형태가 관건

S + V + O + OC
"O가 OC이다/하다"의 관계. 동사에 따라 목적격 보어의 형태가 정해진다.
동사목적격 보어예
call/make/find/name명사·형용사made me happy
want/ask/tell/allow/expectto부정사told me to wait
사역 make/have/let원형부정사made him go
help원형 / tohelped me (to) move
지각 see/hear/watch/feel원형/-ing/p.p.saw her cross/crossing
get은 준사역 → to부정사
get + O + to부정사(got him to help). 수동은 get + O + p.p.(got it done).

2. to부정사 핵심

항목내용
의미상 주어일반 for + 목적격 / 성품 형용사 of + 목적격 (kind of you)
가주어–진주어It is hard to master English.
too ~ to"너무 ~해서 …못한다" too young to drive
형/부 + enough to"…할 만큼 충분히" old enough to vote

3. 특수구문 — 강조·도치·동격

구문예
It ~ that 강조It was John that broke it.
동사 강조I do love you.
부정어 도치Never have I seen …
동격 thatthe fact that he lied
부분 부정not all / not always
강조 It~that vs 가주어 It~that
강조구문은 It is/was·that을 빼도 성립(강조어를 제자리로): It was yesterday that we met → We met yesterday.
▶ 연습문제 (17문항)
유형 A. 5형식 목적격 보어 [1~7]
  1. The teacher had the students ( write / to write ) an essay.
  2. I saw a stranger ( enter / to enter ) the building.
  3. My parents let me ( stay / to stay ) up late.
  4. She got her brother ( fix / to fix ) the computer.
  5. He made his son ( clean / to clean ) the room.
  6. We expect them ( arrive / to arrive ) soon.
  7. I heard my name ( call / called ) from behind.
유형 B. to부정사 [8~12]
  1. It was very kind ( for / of ) you to say so.
  2. This box is too heavy ( to / for ) me to lift.
  3. He is ( enough strong / strong enough ) to lift it.
  4. It is important ( for / of ) students to sleep well.
  5. She was the first ( arriving / to arrive ) at the party.
유형 C. 특수구문 [13~15]
  1. ( It / That ) was in Paris that they first met.
  2. Not until midnight ( he came / did he come ) home.
  3. I ( do / am ) believe in your talent.
유형 D. 문장 완성·전환 [16~17]
  1. 가주어 구문: ______ is difficult ______ (master) a foreign language.
  2. 강조구문으로: We first met in 2010. (in 2010 강조)
