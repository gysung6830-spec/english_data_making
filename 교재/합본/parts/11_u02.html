UNIT 2

관계사

관계대명사·관계부사 완전정복 — 두 문장을 하나로 잇는 연결어

1. 원리: 접속사 + (대)명사·부사

관계사 = 접속사와 (대)명사·부사를 한 몸에 겸한 말
두 문장이 같은 명사를 공유할 때 반복 명사를 관계사로 바꿔 한 문장으로 합친다. 관계사절은 앞의 명사(선행사)를 꾸민다.
I know a man. + He speaks five languages. → I know a man who speaks five languages.
who = and he (접속사+대명사). 선행사 = a man.

2. 관계대명사 — 격과 선택

선행사주격목적격소유격
사람whowho(m)whose
사물·동물whichwhichwhose / of which
공통thatthat—
선행사 포함what (= the thing which) — 선행사를 따로 두지 않음
목적격 관계대명사는 생략 가능
「선행사 + 주어 + 동사」 배열이면 목적격이 생략된 것. the book which I bought

3. 관계부사 & 결정적 판별

관계부사선행사= 전치사+which
whenthe time/dayon/at/in which
wherethe placein/at which
whythe reasonfor which
howthe wayin which (the way how ✕)
뒤 절이 완전한가 불완전한가
관계사 뒤 절에서 주어·목적어가 빠졌으면(불완전) 관계대명사, 다 갖췄으면(완전) 관계부사.
· the house ___ I bought (목적어 없음) → which/that · the house ___ I live (완전) → where

4. 용법·특수 관계사

한정적 vs 계속적(콤마)
콤마 뒤 계속적 용법은 that 불가, 생략 불가. 계속적 which는 앞 문장 전체도 받는다: He passed, which surprised us.
that 선호 / what / 복합관계사
· 최상급·서수·the only/all/every/no + 선행사 → that
· 선행사 없이 "~하는 것" → what (= the thing which)
· whatever/whoever/however = "~든지 / 아무리 ~해도"
▶ 연습문제 (16문항)
유형 A. 빈칸에 알맞은 관계사 [1~6]
  1. This is the village ______ my grandmother was born.
  2. The man ______ car was stolen called the police.
  3. ______ he wants is a little respect.
  4. She is the only person ______ can solve this.
  5. He explained the reason ______ he was absent.
  6. I still remember the day ______ we first met.
유형 B. 어법 선택 [7~11]
  1. The house ( which / where ) I grew up is now a museum.
  2. He has three sisters, ( who / that ) all live abroad.
  3. Tell me the way ( how you did it / you did it ).
  4. This is the book ( that / what ) changed my life.
  5. The city ( which / in which ) he lives is very old.
유형 C. 밑줄 부분 O/X — 틀리면 수정 [12~14]
  1. I have a friend, that lives in Paris.
  2. The reason why she left is unclear.
  3. He told me the news, what made me happy.
유형 D. 두 문장을 관계사로 연결 [15~16]
  1. I met a woman. + Her son teaches at my school.
  2. He passed the driving test. + It made his parents proud.
