영문법 핵심교재 · 최종 점검

10강 · 전범위 누적 최종모의고사

1강 정동사/준동사 ~ 9강 5형식·특수구문 전 범위 통합 20문항
문항 수 20문항 구성 Part A 어법 선택(10) · Part B 틀린 곳 수정(6) · Part C 영작·문장완성(4) 권장 시간 25분
Part A. 어법상 알맞은 것을 고르시오 (1~10)
  1. The scientist studying rare birds ( has / having ) published many papers.1강
  2. This is the reason ( why / which ) she resigned so suddenly.2강
  3. The bridge ( building / built ) in 1920 is still in use.3강
  4. Neither the manager nor the workers ( was / were ) satisfied.4강
  5. The weather in Seoul is colder than ( London / that of London ).5강
  6. Each student must submit ( their / his or her ) own report.6강
  7. The soup smells ( wonderful / wonderfully ) today.7강
  8. ( Despite / Although ) the heavy traffic, we arrived on time.8강
  9. My coach made me ( run / to run ) ten laps.9강
  10. Only after the exam ( I realized / did I realize ) my mistake.4·9강
Part B. 밑줄 친 부분 중 어법상 틀린 곳을 찾아 바르게 고치시오 (11~16)
  1. Walking along the beach, the sunset was breathtaking.3강
  2. The number of applicants have doubled this year.4강
  3. She enjoys reading, painting, and to travel.5강
  4. I have two dogs; one is white and another is black.6강
  5. He drove very careful and slow on the icy road.7강
  6. In spite of it was raining, the match went on.8강
Part C. 지시에 따라 문장을 완성하시오 (17~20)
  1. 두 문장을 관계사로 연결: I met a woman. + Her son teaches at my school.2강
  2. 분사구문으로 전환: Because he was tired, he went to bed early.3강
  3. 빈칸 완성(가주어 구문): ______ is difficult ______ (master) a foreign language.9강
  4. 강조구문으로: We first met in 2010. → (in 2010을 강조)9강

정답 & 해설

Part A

#정답해설
1has주절의 정동사가 필요. studying은 분사(수식) → 뼈대 동사 has.
2why뒤 절(she resigned)이 완전 + 선행사 reason → 관계부사 why.
3built다리가 '지어진'(수동) → 과거분사. 뒤에 정동사 is 존재.
4wereneither A nor B는 B(the workers, 복수)에 일치.
5that of London날씨끼리 비교 → that(=the weather) of London.
6his or hereach student(단수) → 단수 대명사(규범 문법).
7wonderfulsmell은 감각동사 → 형용사 보어.
8Despite뒤가 명사구(the heavy traffic) → 전치사 Despite.
9run사역동사 make + O + 원형부정사.
10did I realizeOnly + 부사구가 문두 → 주어·동사 도치.

Part B

#수정해설
11the sunset → 주어를 I로
(Walking …, I found the sunset breathtaking)		현수분사 오류. 걷는 주체가 the sunset이 되면 안 됨 → 주절 주어를 사람으로.
12have → hasthe number of ~ = "~의 수" → 단수.
13to travel → traveling동명사(reading, painting)와 병렬.
14another → the other둘 중 하나(one)의 나머지 하나 → the other.
15and slow → and slowly동사 drove 수식 부사 carefully와 병렬 → slowly. (careful도 carefully로)
16it was raining → the rain
(또는 In spite of → Although)		in spite of는 전치사 → 뒤에 절 불가.

Part C

#정답 예시해설
17I met a woman whose son teaches at my school.Her → 소유격 관계대명사 whose + 명사(son).
18(Being) Tired, he went to bed early.
= Feeling tired, …		접속사·주어 제거 후 능동은 -ing. Being은 생략 가능.
19It is difficult to master a foreign language.가주어 It + 진주어 to부정사.
20It was in 2010 that we first met.It was ~ that 강조구문으로 시점(in 2010) 강조.
